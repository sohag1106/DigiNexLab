# BrightSkyIT — 100-City Expansion Research
**Goal:** 100 city pages (`/web-design-<city>`) + 100 supporting blog articles, ranked by how fast we can realistically rank (`<city> web design`, `web design company in <city>`, long-tails).
**Date:** 2026-09-21. Method: live SERP counts of genuinely-local competitors per city (Brave/DDG fetches), prior Oman research (2026-09-18), agency-economics and GTLD-fit reasoning where SERPs were bot-walled.

---

## How to read the tiers

- **Tier 1 — Fast wins (rank in weeks):** near-zero actually-local competition in the SERP (validated: Sur, Nizwa, RAK, Fujairah, UAQ). These are Dubai/Riyadh/other-country shops marketing in with landing pages. A genuinely local page with local proof wins fast.
- **Tier 2 — Medium (2–4 months):** a handful of locals, but most are small shops with weak SEO (few backlinks, thin content). Validated: Ajman/Sharjah, Manama, Kuwait City, Dammam/Khobar.
- **Tier 3 — Competitive (4–8 months):** healthy local agency scenes, but many are small/low-SEO-strength. Validated: Jeddah, Riyadh (many low-quality locals), Karachi, Dhaka.
- **Tier 4 — Hard (6–12+ months):** mature markets with strong local agencies + directory dominance (Clutch/GoodFirms/Sortlist own the SERP). Validated: Doha, London, KL, Penang.
- **Never** — no reason for a business there to hire us / we can't plausibly serve: skip.

**Deploy rule (matches repo content rule):** never clone market paragraphs; each city page needs its own genuinely-local content. Proof beats volume — start Tier 1 cities where a case study/contact exists, then radiate.

## Long-tail strategy (applies to all 100)

Each city gets **1 hub page + 2–4 spoke articles**. Spoke titles follow proven patterns — swap the city in:

1. **Cost:** `How much does a website cost in <city>? <YEAR> price guide`
2. **Best-of listicle:** `<n> best web design ideas for <industry> businesses in <city>` (listicles of *ideas*, not agencies — never invite comparison shopping)
3. **Vertical:** `Web design for <clinics | restaurants | real estate | hotels | logistics> in <city>`
4. **Bilingual/Arabic:** `Do you need an Arabic website in <city>? RTL design guide`
5. **Comparison:** `Freelancer vs agency for web design in <city> — honest comparison`
6. **Local proof:** `How we built a <industry> website for a <city> company` (only where real work exists)
7. **E-commerce:** `Selling online from <city>: e-commerce guide for <city> SMEs`

Every spoke article links to the city hub with exact-match anchor (`web design <city>`); hub links back contextually. This is the hub-and-spoke already working in `city-data.js` + `blog-data.js`.

## Rollout plan (matches repo checklist)

Phase by tier, not all at once — Google trusts a site adding 3–5 pages/week more than one that dumps 100:

1. **Weeks 1–4:** Tier 1 cities 1–15 (one per day max, 5/week). Update `city-data.js`, routes in `src/main.jsx`, redirects in `public/_redirects`, `public/sitemap.xml` each batch.
2. **Weeks 5–8:** Tier 1 #16–30 + first 10 spokes for the validated zero-competition cities.
3. **Months 3–4:** Tier 2, plus GBP posts per city (Service-Area Business covers all cities from one profile).
4. **Months 5–12:** Tier 3–4 opportunistically — only after Search Console shows the Tier 1–2 pages actually ranking.

**Per-city QA gate (do not skip):** search `<city> web design` in a fresh/incognito SERP before writing. If the local pack + page 1 shows 3+ real local agencies with strong content, downgrade the city to a later tier and do its long-tail spokes first.
